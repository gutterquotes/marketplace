## Local modifiers

Combine core services with actual supported cities, states, and ZIP-led matching. Current city templates cover major markets in Georgia, North Carolina, South Carolina, Tennessee, Alabama, Florida, Louisiana, and Mississippi. Each city page should link to its service pages and each service-city page should contain local context, not merely a swapped city name.

## Page quality rules

1. Match one distinct homeowner intent per page.
2. Put the exact primary phrase in the title, H1, description, and natural opening copy.
3. Answer the query before presenting the call to action.
4. Explain project inputs, professional recommendations, workmanship, drainage planning, warranties, and scheduling.
5. Link to the quote form, related service pages, useful guides, and supported locations.
6. Include Article or Service, FAQ, Breadcrumb, and organization structured data where appropriate.
7. Do not invent local offices, contractor counts, ratings, prices, licenses, or availability.
8. Do not publish near-duplicate city pages without useful local and service-specific information.
